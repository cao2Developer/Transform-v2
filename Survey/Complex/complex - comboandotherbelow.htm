Instructions

Click the Add button to create a new entry

Fill in the form with the relevant information

Click the tick to store the entry (it will appear in the history)

You cannot add any more records

The maximum number of records has been reached, you cannot create any more. You can, however, contineue edit the existing records.

To edit, click on the record in the history, the record can then be changed

To delete, click on the record in the history and use the delete button, you will need to press the delete button twice to confirm the deletion

History

  • You can enter a maximum of four entries.
  • Entries are listed in reverse order of entry
  • Entries can be deleted, if no longer required
